About Innate & MARS

Innate is building the world's first open-source personal AI robot platform. MARS combines NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano compute with a 5+1 DOF arm and BASIC agentic OS to reason and act in real-world environments.

Open-Source Core

Hardware schematics, 3D CAD files, and BASIC OS behavior libraries are 100% open-source under MIT license.

Fast Teleoperation

Collect imitation learning datasets using any smartphone mounted to the teleop arm in under 30 minutes.

Community Platform

Share physical skills, download community behaviors, and train AI models together.
